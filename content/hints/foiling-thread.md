---
title: 'Foiling a threat'
topics:
  - Festivities
  - Threats
order: 30
---

After resolving scored **mollies and desires** at a festivity, foil each scored threat:

1. Add the threat to the **safe pile**.
2. The player who played it adds **all matching cards from their reputation** to the safe pile.

If the **community played the threat**, every player adds all matching cards from their reputation to the safe pile, in turn order starting with the **festivity runner**.

**The Pride Rule:** Each player who adds reputation cards this way may take a matching suit token for each card they add. This is **all or nothing**: take a token for every card added, or take no tokens.

If multiple threats are scored, the **festivity runner chooses the order** in which they are foiled.
