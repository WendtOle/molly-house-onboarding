**Infamy counts card values. Reputation counts cards and suit tokens.** Calculate both for the **relevant suit**, not across all suits.

## Who is most infamous?

Add up the numerical values of the matching cards in each player's reputation. **Each molly counts as 10. Suit tokens do not count.** The player with the highest total is the most infamous.

When a market threat is added to gossip, the most infamous player in its suit is exposed. If players tie, all tied players are exposed. If nobody has a matching reputation card, nobody is exposed.

## How much reputation do you have?

Count your **matching cards + matching suit tokens**. Each counts as 1, regardless of a card's value. A molly also counts as just 1 card here.

**Example:** A 2, a 7, a molly, and two suit tokens, all in the same suit, give you **19 infamy** (2 + 7 + 10) and **5 reputation** (3 cards + 2 tokens).
