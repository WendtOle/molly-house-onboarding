When resolving a **market discard**, take the market card **farthest from the vice deck**:

- **Desire → safe pile.**
- **Molly → gossip pile.**
- **Threat → gossip pile, then compare infamy.**

For a threat, each player totals the values of the cards in their reputation **matching the threat's suit**. Mollies count as **10**; suit tokens do not count.

The **most infamous player is exposed**. If players tie for the highest total, all tied players are exposed. If nobody has a matching reputation card, nobody is exposed.

**A molly going to gossip does not trigger an infamy comparison.**
